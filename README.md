## 🎧 Getting started with the sound

Once you launch the app, you need to connect your hardware. The software needs an audio interface to process your guitar signal. 

1. Connect your guitar to the input on your audio interface.
2. Open the Settings menu in the top corner of the app.
3. Select your audio device from the list.
4. Choose the input channel where you plugged in your guitar.
5. Set your output to your headphones or speakers.

You should hear your guitar signal immediately. Adjust the input gain slider to avoid distortion at the clean stage. The neural model provides the drive you need. Use the parametric controls to shape the frequency response of the amp.

## 🎛️ Understanding the features

The interface provides three primary control areas:

- Input Gain: This adjusts the signal level entering the neural engine. Higher settings create more crunch and power tube saturation.
- Neural Model Selector: This allows you to choose different amp profiles. Each profile represents a specific type of vacuum tube amplifier.
- Parametric EQ: This section lets you change the tone. Use the frequency dial to pick a range and the gain dial to cut or boost that area.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting common issues

If you encounter silence, check your audio settings. Ensure the software selects the correct input. If the sound stutters, increase the buffer size in your audio interface control panel. A larger buffer reduces CPU load but adds a small delay to your playing. A smaller buffer provides a real-time feel but requires a faster processor. 

If the application fails to launch, confirm that you installed the latest drivers for your audio hardware. Old drivers cause conflicts with audio plugins.
